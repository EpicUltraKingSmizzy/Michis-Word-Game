# Grid statistics and minimum word counts

Measured on 2,000 randomly rolled boards per setting with the game's real dice and dictionary (SCOWL 70, US + UK, 125,427 words after the blocklist). Generated by `dart run tool/grid_stats.dart 2000` (random seed 42). "Words" counts distinct valid words of at least the shortest-word length; "points" is the total score if one player found every word (letters − 2 per word). A *dead letter* is a tile that isn't part of any valid word.

## Chosen minimums and the cost of the filter

A board is dealt only if it has **no dead letters** and at least the minimum number of words below. Generation rolls up to 300 boards and otherwise falls back to the best one seen (fewest dead letters, then most words); in these runs the cap was never hit.

## Recommendation and reasoning

- **3- and 4-letter minimums use about the 10th percentile** of the unfiltered word count (4x4: 40 / 20, 5x5: 130 / 80, 6x6: 250 / 175). That cuts the thin boards that feel like there's nothing to find, while still allowing variety; the dead-letter rule already removes most low-count boards.
- **5-letter minimums are a bit higher than the 10th percentile** (4x4: 8, 5x5: 30, 6x6: 75). On a 4x4 the 10th percentile is only 4 words, which isn't a fun round, so 8 (about the 25th percentile) is used there; 5x5 and 6x6 sit near the 10th percentile.
- **Dead letters are the stronger filter.** 27–78% of random boards have at least one unusable letter (most often with a 5-letter minimum on a 4x4), so rejecting them changes the feel more than the word-count minimum does. Generation stays fast: 1.5–4.2 rolls on average, at most 26 in 18,000 deals.
- To change any of this, edit `lib/logic/grid_rules.dart` (`minWordsTable`, `rejectDeadLetters`, `maxBoardRolls`) and rerun `dart run tool/grid_stats.dart` to see the effect.
